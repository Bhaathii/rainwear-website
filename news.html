Queen's Awards for Innovation & Enterprise

Rainwear is proud to have received two prestigious Queen's Awards - the highest official UK awards for British businesses:

  • 2006 - Queen's Award for Innovation: Recognized for our revolutionary Seam Sealing Machine that transformed waterproof garment manufacturing.
  • 2008 - Queen's Award for Enterprise: Acknowledged for our successful international business ventures and contribution to exports.

These awards recognize our commitment to innovation, quality, and business excellence. As the operational arm of Ardmel Group UK, we continue to uphold the standards that earned us these prestigious honors.
