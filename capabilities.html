Innovation & Technology

System Dual Protection (SDP)

We have developed an award-winning System Dual Protection (SDP) which uses a simple 2-layer concept that manages inner condensation accumulating inside garments during wet and cold conditions.

  • 100% waterproof outer layer - tough and durable
  • Lightweight inner layer with hydrophilic membrane
  • Rapidly transports moisture away from the body
  • Queen's Award winning innovation
