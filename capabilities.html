Design & Development Services

Our flagship technologies that set us apart in technical garment manufacturing

01

Seam Sealing (Hot Air & Hot Wedge)

Flagship Technology

Our flagship technology involves applying a specialized tape over a stitched seam to prevent water or air from leaking through the needle holes — the critical last step in making a truly waterproof garment.

The Process

A precision-controlled stream of hot air (or a heated wedge) melts the adhesive on a sealing tape. The tape is then pressed onto the seam by specialized rollers, creating a completely waterproof seal.

Key Innovation — Digital Control Systems

Our Digital Control Systems allow for minute adjustments in temperature and speed, which is critical when working with heat-sensitive fabrics like ultra-lightweight nylons or heavy-duty canvases.

02

Ultrasonic Bonding & Cutting

Advanced Technology

Instead of using heat from an external element, this technology uses high-frequency acoustic vibrations to join and cut synthetic fabrics with unmatched precision.

Ultrasonic Welding

The vibrations create friction at the molecular level between two pieces of synthetic fabric, melting them together without the need for glue or thread — creating invisible, durable bonds.

Cutting & Sealing

Ultrasonic horns cut fabric and seal the edges simultaneously, preventing fraying — a common issue with traditional blade cutting on synthetic materials.

03

Seamless Bonding (Adhesive Films)

Premium Technology

Often seen in high-end sportswear and "tech-wear," this technology uses thermal adhesive films to join fabrics — producing clean, seamless finishes with superior flexibility.

Hot Melt Technology

Solid adhesive films or tapes are activated using heat presses, creating bonds that are flexible, durable, and invisible from the outside.

Precision Pressing

Our flatbed and vacuum presses ensure even pressure and temperature distribution, preventing "strike-through" (where glue seeps through the fabric) and ensuring a flexible, durable bond.

04

Computerized Strip & Laser Cutting

Precision Technology

To ensure precision before the joining process, we use automated cutting technologies that deliver exact dimensions and sealed edges every time.

Cold/Hot Strip Cutting

Programmed machines that cut elastics, webbing, and Velcro to exact lengths with specific end-seals — eliminating manual error and material waste.

Laser Technology

Used for intricate patterns and ensuring edges are cauterized (sealed) as they are cut, which is vital for automotive filters and technical apparel construction.

System Dual Protection (SDP)

We have developed an award-winning System Dual Protection (SDP) which uses a simple 2-layer concept that manages inner condensation accumulating inside garments during wet and cold conditions.

  • 100% waterproof outer layer - tough and durable
  • Lightweight inner layer with hydrophilic membrane
  • Rapidly transports moisture away from the body
  • Queen's Award winning innovation
