Our History

Rainwear (Pvt) Ltd was established in 1989 by Mr. Rube Fernando with a vision to manufacture world-class waterproof garments in Sri Lanka. From humble beginnings, the company has grown to become the No.1 ranked waterproof garment manufacturer in the country.

As the operational arm of the Ardmel Group of companies, UK, Rainwear holds the patent rights for the world's first bonding technology. This groundbreaking innovation has positioned us at the forefront of technical apparel manufacturing.

  • Founded in 1989 by Mr. Rube Fernando
  • Operational arm of Ardmel Group UK
  • Patent holder for first bonding technology
  • Located in Biyagama Export Processing Zone

Our Mission & Vision

Guiding principles that drive our excellence

Our Mission

Our mission is to delight our customers by providing total solutions, recognizing their needs and carrying them throughout our process to develop innovative products suitable for ever-changing consumer requirements, while keeping our loyal customer base by maintaining high quality standards and on-time delivery through creative and dedicated staff.

Our Vision

Our vision is to be the first choice for customers and be the creative center for excellence in the clothing industry. We aspire to lead through innovation, quality, and sustainable practices that set new standards in waterproof garment manufacturing.

Our Business

Comprehensive apparel manufacturing services from design to dispatch

What We Do

We specialize in the design, manufacture, and dispatch of apparel and accessories using cutting-edge technologies:

  • Sewing, welding, and bonding technologies
  • Seam sealing capabilities
  • Woven, non-woven, and knitted materials
  • Waterproof, fire, and chemical resistant materials

Quality Policy

We at Rainwear (Pvt) Ltd are committed to manufacture and supply specialized apparel products to international standards and consistently meet with our customers' requirements for quality, safety and delivery. To meet these objectives we are committed to establish and maintain a quality management system complying with ISO 9001:2015 international standards, set quality objectives at all relevant levels and to review these objectives at planned intervals for continual improvement.

Ethical Policy

Rainwear is committed to maintaining high standards of social and ethical business performance through integrity, transparency, and continuous improvement. We are committed to equal opportunity employment and the principles of fair trade, wages, and ethical investment. Our manufacturing processes are free from abuse of basic human rights, ensuring safe working conditions and fair wage rates with no use of child or forced labor.
