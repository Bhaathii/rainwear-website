Health & Safety Policy

Ensuring a safe and healthy workplace for all

Our Commitment to Safety

Rainwear (Pvt) Ltd recognizes that the safety of all staff, customers, and visitors is of paramount importance and accepts its responsibility for providing a safe and healthy workplace.

The Board of Directors pursues a policy statement to promote health and safety at work and seeks the cooperation of all employees for that purpose. The Company provides working conditions which comply with relevant statutory requirements and officially approved codes of practice.

Key Policy Areas:

  • Adequate control of health and safety risks
  • Monitor effectiveness of health and safety provisions
  • Provide and maintain safe plant and equipment
  • Ensure safe handling and use of substances
  • Provide necessary information, instruction, and training
  • Prevent accidents and work-related ill health

Our Environmental Commitment

Rainwear aims to ensure statutory provisions of environmental protection are observed. Environmental impacts shall be minimized more than stipulated by law as far as it is economically reasonable. We remain alert and responsive to developing issues, knowledge, and public concerns, while ensuring all employees are aware of our environmental policy and motivated to apply it.

Ethical Policy

Maintaining the highest standards of social and ethical business performance

Our Ethical Standards

Rainwear is committed to maintaining high standards of social and ethical business performance through an integrated framework of accountability and active engagement, based on the principles of integrity, transparency, and continuous improvement.

Our Commitments:

  • Equal Opportunity: Employment without regard to color, race, nationality, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or marital status
  • Fair Trade: Committed to principles of fair trade, wages, and ethical investment
  • No Child Labor: Manufacturing processes free from child labor
  • No Forced Labor: All workers employed voluntarily
  • Safe Conditions: Working conditions are safe with fair wage rates

Garments Without Guilt

Rainwear proudly abides by the charter on GARMENTS WITHOUT GUILT as laid out by the Joint Apparel Association Forum of Sri Lanka (JAAF), which requires members to conduct their business in an environment-friendly and ethical manner that is:

Free of Child Labour
Free of Forced Labour
Free of Discrimination
Free of Sweatshop Practices
