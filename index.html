Sri Lanka's No.1 Waterproof Garment Manufacturer

Established in 1989 by Mr. Rube Fernando, Rainwear (Pvt) Ltd has grown to become the leading waterproof garment manufacturer in Sri Lanka. As the operational arm of Ardmel Group UK, we hold the patent rights for the world's first bonding technology.

  • Queens Award for Technological Innovation (2006)
  • Queens Award for Enterprise (2008)
