Innovation

Our Core Technologies

Flagship technologies driving our design & development capabilities

01

Seam Sealing (Hot Air & Hot Wedge)

Flagship Technology

Our flagship technology — applying specialized tape over stitched seams with precision-controlled hot air or heated wedge to create completely waterproof seals.

Digital Control Systems

Minute adjustments in temperature and speed — critical for heat-sensitive fabrics like ultra-lightweight nylons or heavy-duty canvases.

02

Ultrasonic Bonding & Cutting

Advanced Technology

High-frequency acoustic vibrations create molecular-level bonds between synthetic fabrics — no glue or thread needed. Simultaneous cutting and edge sealing prevents fraying.

Molecular-Level Welding

Vibrations create friction that melts synthetic fabrics together for invisible, durable bonds without adhesives.

03

Seamless Bonding (Adhesive Films)

Premium Technology

Thermal adhesive film technology for high-end sportswear and tech-wear — flatbed and vacuum presses ensure strike-through-free, flexible, durable bonds.

Precision Hot Melt Pressing

Even pressure and temperature distribution prevents glue seep-through for a clean, professional finish.

04

Computerized Strip & Laser Cutting

Precision Technology

Automated cutting technologies ensuring exact dimensions before the joining process — cold/hot strip cutting and laser cutting with cauterized edges.

Laser & Programmed Cutting

Intricate patterns with sealed edges — vital for automotive filters and technical apparel construction.
